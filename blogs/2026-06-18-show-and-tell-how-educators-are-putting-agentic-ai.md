Educators are moving beyond theoretical discussions of agentic AI to actively building and deploying AI agents in real classrooms, sharing their experiences at conferences and symposiums. This "show-and-tell phase" represents a shift where practical classroom implementation now outpaces institutional policy, with universities like Sydney, ASU, and Michigan leading collaborative efforts to demonstrate what works.
